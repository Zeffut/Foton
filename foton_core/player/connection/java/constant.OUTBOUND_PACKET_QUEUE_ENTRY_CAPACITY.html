Secondary bound for empty and tiny reliable packets, whose allocation overhead is not encoded bytes.

What a burst leaves in the queue has to fit, because a full queue closes the connection, and vanilla never does: Netty buffers what a slow client has not read. Each entity that appears costs five entries (a bundle’s two delimiters, the spawn, its metadata and its velocity), and each primed TNT then costs three a tick (its fuse, its position and its velocity) until it goes off. Orbital Strike Cannon’s biggest nuke is 1956 TNT on a 60 tick fuse, about 352000 entries for each player in range; a client on a home connection, or one that Via translates, drains that in seconds and the queue is full before it does. 393216 holds it.

An entry costs about 400 bytes in memory (measured), so this is up to ~150 MB for one client that has stopped reading; GLOBAL_OUTBOUND_ENTRY_BUDGET bounds all of them together.
