Resolves an enchantment’s entity target against a post-attack context.

Vanilla parity: the parameters Enchantment.damageContext puts in the loot context. this is the victim, always – not whoever carries the enchanted item. Bane of Arthropods is the reason that matters: it sits on the attacker’s sword and asks this whether it is an arthropod, meaning the entity being hit.
