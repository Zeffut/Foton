Events about a player arriving and leaving.

These two first because they are what the ecosystem asks for most: of the fifty-nine most-downloaded server plugins surveyed in dev/plugin-api-usage.json, forty need the join and thirty-six the quit. Two events reach two thirds of that corpus, which is why the measurement came before the design.
