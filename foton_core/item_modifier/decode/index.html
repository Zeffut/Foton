Module decode

Decoding the item-modifier codec.

Vanilla parity: LootItemFunctions.ROOT_CODEC over NumberProviders.CODEC. Errors are plain strings: the caller wraps them in whichever error its source needs (a datapack load message, or argument.resource_or_id).
