The enchantments an enchanting table is allowed to roll.

Vanilla parity: EnchantmentTags.IN_ENCHANTING_TABLE, which the menu passes to selectEnchantment instead of the whole registry. It is why Mending and the curses never appear at a table however many shelves surround it.
