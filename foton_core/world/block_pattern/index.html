Matching multi-block shapes against the world. Matching multi-block shapes against the world.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.level.block.state.pattern.BlockPattern, BlockPatternBuilder and BlockInWorld. Vanilla builds patterns for the carved pumpkin’s three golems, for the wither, and for the end crystal respawn ritual, so this lives next to the world rather than inside any one block behavior.
