Natural mob spawning.

Vanilla parity: NaturalSpawner, with one documented structural difference. Vanilla walks every ticking chunk and applies a per-chunk cap; Foton samples candidate positions in the ring around each player instead, because chunk ticking does not expose the per-chunk mob accounting vanilla relies on. The observable rules that matter are reproduced: the 24-block exclusion around players, the 128-block outer limit, the per-category budget, where each kind of mob is allowed to stand, and the spawn rules the mob itself imposes.
