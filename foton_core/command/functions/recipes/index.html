Crafting recipes read from datapacks.

Vanilla parity: RecipeManager over Recipe.CODEC, restricted to minecraft:crafting_shaped and minecraft:crafting_shapeless, the two the runtime registry can take after it froze. Any other type is refused with its name instead of being skipped, so a pack that depends on one says so in the log. An ingredient in the pre-1.21.2 {"item": ...} form is refused too, as vanilla refuses it.
