Village raids.

Vanilla parity: the net.minecraft.world.entity.raid package.

A raid is the only vanilla event that pulls five systems together at once: it lives in the point-of-interest index (a village is an occupied village POI), in saved data (one Raids per loaded world), on the boss bar, in the wave spawner and in the mobs themselves. Raid is the state machine, Raids is the per-world manager that ticks and persists it, and crate::entity::raider is the seam the mobs read it through.

The two entry points are the Raid Omen effect running out on a player standing in a village – see [crate::entity::living_base] – and the /raid start command.
