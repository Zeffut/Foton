foton_core::raid

Module raids 

Source
Expand description

Every raid one loaded world is running.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.raid.Raids, a SavedData stored per dimension under data/raids.dat. Foton writes the same thing to data/raids.toml through SavedDataManager, the way chunk_tickets is written – one file per loaded world, not one per domain, because a raid belongs to the dimension its village is in.

What is persisted is exactly what vanilla’s codec persists: the raid’s counters, its center and the players who earned Hero of the Village. The raiders themselves are not, because each raider saves its own RaidId and puts itself back into its wave when its chunk loads.
