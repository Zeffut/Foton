Campfire block entity implementation.

A campfire holds four items on its edge and cooks each one on its own timer. It is deliberately not a Container: vanilla’s CampfireBlockEntity implements Clearable and nothing else, so a hopper cannot feed it and a comparator cannot read it. Food goes on one item at a time by hand, and comes off as a dropped item entity.
