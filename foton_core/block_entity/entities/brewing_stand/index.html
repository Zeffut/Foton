Brewing stand block entity.

Vanilla parity: BrewingStandBlockEntity. Three bottles brew at once from one ingredient, which is what makes brewing worth the walk to the nether: the ingredient is spent once and every bottle it applies to is converted. Bottles it does not apply to are left alone rather than blocking the brew.
