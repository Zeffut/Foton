Crafter block entity.

Vanilla parity: CrafterBlockEntity. Nine slots, but unlike every other nine-slot block each one can be switched off, and a slot that is off counts as full: it takes no items and it still feeds a comparator. That is the whole point of the block – it lets a recipe keep a hole in the middle of its grid while a hopper pours items in from above.
