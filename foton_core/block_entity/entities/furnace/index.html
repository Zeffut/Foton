Module furnace

Furnace, smoker and blast furnace block entity.

Vanilla parity: AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity. The three variants share this implementation and differ only by which recipe family they look up and by a burn-duration multiplier, exactly as the vanilla subclasses do.
