Banner block entity.

Vanilla parity: BannerBlockEntity. A banner’s color is in the block, but its pattern layers and its name are not – they live here, and they are the only reason a placed banner is anything but a colored sheet.

Without this the loom’s output is a dead end: a banner stamped with six layers becomes a plain one the moment it is put down, and the item that comes back when it is broken has lost them too.
