Module hopper

Hopper block entity.

Vanilla parity: HopperBlockEntity. A hopper pushes one item per transfer into whatever container it points at, pulls one item out of the container above it, and swallows item entities resting in its bowl. Everything it does is one item at a time, gated by an eight-tick cooldown.
