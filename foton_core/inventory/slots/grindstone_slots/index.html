The grindstone’s two inputs and its result.

Vanilla parity: the anonymous slots of GrindstoneMenu. A grindstone does two unrelated things through one pair of slots – it strips enchantments off a single item, and it welds two damaged copies of the same item into one – and in both cases it keeps the curses, which is the whole reason a cursed item cannot be laundered.
