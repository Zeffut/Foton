Module loom_slots 

Source
Expand description

The loom’s three inputs and its result.

Vanilla parity: the anonymous slots of LoomMenu. A loom takes a banner, a dye and optionally a pattern item, and stamps one more layer onto the banner. Which layer is not decided by the inputs: the player picks it from the list the pattern item offers, which is why the result depends on a selection as well as on the slots.
