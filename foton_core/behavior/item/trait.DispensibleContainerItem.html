Expand description

An item a dispenser can empty with nobody holding it.

Vanilla parity: the DispensibleContainerItem interface, implemented by BucketItem – and so by MobBucketItem – and by SolidBucketItem. Its whole point is the nullable user: the behavior registered for every filled bucket calls emptyContents(null, ..), so nothing reached from here may ask for a player.

fn check_extra_content( &self, _user: Option<&Player>, _world: &Arc<World>, _stack: &ItemStack, _pos: BlockPos, )

Releases whatever the container carries beyond its fluid.

Vanilla parity: DispensibleContainerItem.checkExtraContent, whose only override is the one that lets the fish out of a mob bucket. Vanilla runs it at the position emptyContents was asked for, not the one the fluid may have retried into.
