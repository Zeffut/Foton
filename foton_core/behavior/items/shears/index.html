Vanilla ShearsItem.

Only the two rules that live on the item class are here. The shearing this item is named for belongs to whatever is being sheared and is already implemented there: sheep and mooshrooms in entity/entities/mobs/passive/, pumpkin carving in blocks/vegetation/pumpkin_block.rs, beehive harvesting in blocks/container/beehive_block.rs, tripwire disarming in blocks/redstone/tripwire/, and the dispenser variants in blocks/container/dispense_behavior.rs. Mining speed comes from the item’s minecraft:tool component, so vanilla’s createToolProperties needs no port.
