foton_core::behavior::items::bundle

The editable view of a bundle’s contents.

Vanilla keeps BundleContents.Mutable inside the component itself. Foton cannot: deciding whether an item may go into a bundle asks the item’s behavior (ItemBehavior::can_fit_inside_container_items), which lives in foton-core, while the component lives in foton-registry. The weight rule therefore stays with the component and the editing lives here.
