Vanilla ShelfBlock behavior.

A shelf displays three items. Clicking a slot swaps that one item with what the player is holding. Powering the shelf instead makes it swap the whole hotbar row at once, and lets up to three shelves in a line act as one nine slot row – that chain is what the SIDE_CHAIN_PART property records.
