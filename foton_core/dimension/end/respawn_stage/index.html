The four-crystal ritual that brings the dragon back.

Vanilla parity: DragonRespawnStage. Vanilla writes this as an enum whose constants each override tick; the Rust form is one match over the same five states, driven by EnderDragonFight::tick.

Every position here is the literal BlockPos(0, 128, 0) vanilla writes, not an offset from the fight origin. That is deliberate on vanilla’s side and kept deliberately: the beams of a respawn always converge on the world origin even when the fight is centered elsewhere.
