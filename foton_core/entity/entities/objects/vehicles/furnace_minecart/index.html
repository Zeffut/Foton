The furnace minecart.

Vanilla parity: MinecartFurnace. A cart that drives itself: feed it coal and it pushes along the rails in whatever direction it was fed from, for a minute per piece of fuel. It is slower than an ordinary cart, which is the price of not needing a powered rail.

Everything about rolling is super::minecart_common; what is here is the fuel, the push, and the slowdown the push replaces.
