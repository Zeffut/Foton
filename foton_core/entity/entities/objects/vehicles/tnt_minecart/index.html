The TNT minecart.

Vanilla parity: MinecartTNT. A cart that carries a charge: an activator rail lights the fuse, and eighty ticks later it goes off. Running into a wall at speed sets it off immediately, and the faster it was going the bigger the blast – which is what makes a TNT cart worth more than the TNT in it.

Everything about rolling is super::minecart_common; the explosion is crate::world::World::explode. Both already existed, and neither had a cart to connect them.
