Boats and rafts that carry a chest.

Vanilla parity: ChestBoat and ChestRaft, which are AbstractChestBoat – a boat with twenty-seven slots and one seat instead of two. Everything about floating is super::boat_common, the same code an ordinary boat runs; what is here is the chest and the rule for which of the two a right-click means.
