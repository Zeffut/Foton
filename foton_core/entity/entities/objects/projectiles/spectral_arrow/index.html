Spectral arrow entity.

Vanilla parity: SpectralArrow. An AbstractArrow that flies exactly like a plain one and hands its target ten seconds of glowing on top of the damage. That is the whole class: a duration, a doPostHurtEffects override, and a different pickup item.

The flight body mirrors super::ArrowEntity the way vanilla’s two AbstractArrow subclasses mirror each other; ThrownTridentEntity already carries its own copy for the same reason.
