Evoker fangs entity.

Vanilla parity: EvokerFangs. A pair of jaws that rise out of the ground, bite once, and sink. Vanilla files it under projectiles but it is a plain entity: it does not move, it cannot be hurt, and it exists for a little over a second. The two numbers that matter are the warmup – which is how the evoker’s line of fangs ripples outwards instead of firing at once – and the eight-tick offset at which the bite lands, well after the jaws are visible.
