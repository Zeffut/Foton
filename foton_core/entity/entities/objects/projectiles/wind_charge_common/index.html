What the two wind charges share.

Vanilla parity: AbstractWindCharge, the base a hand-thrown WindCharge and a breeze’s BreezeWindCharge sit on. Both fly dead straight – no gravity, no drag, no acceleration – and burst on the first thing they touch, shoving whatever stands nearby without breaking a block or lighting a fire. Only the size of that burst, how hard it shoves and the noise it makes differ, so those three are what AbstractWindCharge asks a concrete charge for.
