Wither skull.

Vanilla parity: WitherSkull, on AbstractHurtingProjectile. It comes in two forms. The plain skull is what the wither’s side heads spit; the dangerous one is the blue skull the middle head charges up, and it differs in two ways: it keeps less of its speed each tick, so it travels slower and visibly homes less, and it eats through blocks a plain skull cannot.

Both forms wither what they hit, and a kill heals the wither that threw it, which is the whole reason the fight has a healing phase.
