Dragon fireball.

Vanilla parity: DragonFireball, on AbstractHurtingProjectile. It does no damage of its own at all – no onHitEntity override, no explosion. What it does is leave a cloud of dragon’s breath where it lands, and the cloud is the weapon. That is also why the dragon can fire one at its own feet: the ball passes straight through the entity that shot it.
