Thrown splash potion.

Vanilla parity: AbstractThrownPotion and ThrownSplashPotion. Brewing could already turn a potion into a splash potion with gunpowder, and a player could hold one and do nothing with it. This is what the gunpowder was for.

The distance falloff is the whole reason splash potions are a choice rather than a strictly better bottle: full strength only at the center, nothing past four blocks, and a duration cut in proportion.

The lingering bottle is its own entity, super::LingeringPotionEntity, chosen by the item that throws it – not by the bottle this entity carries.
