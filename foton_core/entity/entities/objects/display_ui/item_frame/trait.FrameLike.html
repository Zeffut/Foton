Expand description

The half of ItemFrame that the glow frame inherits in vanilla.

Foton’s two frames are separate structs rather than a subclass pair, so what vanilla gets from extends ItemFrame lives here: the fixed flag, the drop chance, and the damage rules built on them. An implementation supplies the five accessors its own storage decides.

fn drop_frame_contents( &self, world: &World, caused_by: Option<&SharedEntity>, with_frame: bool, )

Drops what a broken or emptied frame leaves behind.

Vanilla parity: the private ItemFrame.dropItem(level, causedBy, withFrame). A fixed frame drops nothing and keeps its item; a creative player gets nothing back; and the framed item rolls against its own drop chance, which is how a map in a structure frame stays put.

Not implemented: removeFramedMap, the map-tracking bookkeeping. Foton tracks no frames on a map, so there is nothing to remove.

fn drop_broken_frame(&self, world: &World, caused_by: Option<&SharedEntity>)

fn hurt_item_frame(&self, world: &World, source: &DamageSource) -> bool

Vanilla parity: ItemFrame.hurtServer.

A punch on a frame holding something empties it and leaves the frame on the wall; a punch on an empty one takes the frame down. An explosion skips the emptying and always breaks it.
