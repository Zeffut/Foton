Module armor_stand 

Source
Expand description

The armor stand.

Vanilla parity: ArmorStand. It is a LivingEntity with no AI at all – the first one in Foton that is not a mob – and almost everything it does is in two places: swapping gear when a player right-clicks it, and the two-hit rule that breaks it.

Not implemented: the six pose rotations. They exist in the synced data with vanilla’s defaults, so a stand looks right, but nothing reads or writes them and the Pose tag is neither saved nor loaded – Foton has no NBT codec for Rotations, and a half-written one would lose a map-maker’s work silently.
