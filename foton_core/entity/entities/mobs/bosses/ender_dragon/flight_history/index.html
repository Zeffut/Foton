The dragon’s rolling record of where its body has been.

Vanilla parity: DragonFlightHistory. The dragon’s neck, head and tail do not follow its current position – they follow where it was, five, twelve, fourteen and sixteen ticks ago, which is what makes the body flex when it turns. This is the ring buffer those lookups read.

Gap: vanilla’s get(int, float) interpolates two samples for a partial tick, and copyFrom clones the buffer. Both exist only for the client renderer and its entity render state, so neither is kept here.
