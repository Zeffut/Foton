Tropical fish entity.

Vanilla parity: TropicalFish. What is worth care here is the variant: one int holds the pattern, the base color and the pattern color at once, and the pattern’s own id is itself two fields packed together. Nine spawns in ten take one of twenty-two named combinations; the tenth is rolled freely out of the full two thousand seven hundred and change.
