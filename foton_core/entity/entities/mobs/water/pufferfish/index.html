Pufferfish entity.

Vanilla parity: Pufferfish, AbstractFish and WaterAnimal. A pufferfish is a cod with a threat display: anything scary within two blocks inflates it over two stages, and while it is inflated it stings whatever touches it for damage and poison that both scale with how puffed up it is. Deflating is on a timer of its own, which is why it stays a ball for a while after the swimmer has gone.
