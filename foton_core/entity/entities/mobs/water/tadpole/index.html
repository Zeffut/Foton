Tadpole entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.frog.Tadpole. A tadpole is a fish with a clock: it swims, follows a player holding a slime ball, and after twenty minutes turns into a frog. It is what a FrogspawnBlock hatches into, which is the loop this closes.
