Behavior shared by the squid and the glow squid.

Vanilla puts this in Squid, which the glow squid extends. Foton has no inheritance, so it lives here the way super::fish serves the cod and the salmon: the two entities keep only what actually differs, which for the glow squid is four sounds, a particle, and the dark ticks it counts after being hurt.
