Magma cube entity.

Vanilla parity: MagmaCube, which is AbstractCubeMob with the Nether’s temperament. Nearly everything is super::cube_common; what is genuinely its own is that it is dangerous at every size, armored in proportion to that size, and jumps higher and less often than a slime – a big magma cube crosses ground in long arcs rather than a steady patter.
