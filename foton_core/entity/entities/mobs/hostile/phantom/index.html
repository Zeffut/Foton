Phantom entity.

Vanilla parity: Phantom and its four goals. A phantom never paths: it circles an anchor point high above whatever it is hunting, and every eight to twelve seconds it drops the circle and swoops straight through its target. Everything it does is written into one moveTargetPoint that PhantomMoveControl then flies at.
