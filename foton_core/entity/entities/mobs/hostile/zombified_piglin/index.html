Zombified piglin entity.

Vanilla parity: ZombifiedPiglin. The mob that makes the Nether feel populated rather than hostile: a crowd of them will ignore a player walking straight through, and turn as one the moment any of them is hit. That second half is the point of the mob, and it is two behaviors – the grudge from NeutralMob, and the shout that spreads it.
