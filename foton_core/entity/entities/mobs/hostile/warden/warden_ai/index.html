The warden’s brain.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.warden.WardenAi. Eight activities, and the order they are tried in is the warden’s whole personality: it emerges, then digs away if nothing has happened, then roars, then fights, then goes to look at whatever it heard, then sniffs, then wanders.
