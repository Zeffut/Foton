Vindicator entity.

Vanilla parity: Vindicator. The illager with the axe. Outside a raid it is a plain melee hostile with a patrol; inside one it breaks doors. The one oddity is Johnny: a vindicator renamed with a name tag stops caring what it is attacking and goes after every living thing in range, which is the single most dangerous mob in the game to let loose in a village.
