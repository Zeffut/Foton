Evoker entity.

Vanilla parity: Evoker. The illager that never touches you: it backs away from anything that gets close and answers with fangs from the floor, an escort of vexes, and – with nothing to fight – a blue sheep turned red. All three run on the crate::entity::SpellcasterIllager rhythm.
