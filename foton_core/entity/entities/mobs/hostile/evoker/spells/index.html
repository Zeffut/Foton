The evoker’s three spells.

Vanilla parity: Evoker.EvokerSummonSpellGoal, Evoker.EvokerAttackSpellGoal and Evoker.EvokerWololoSpellGoal. Each is four numbers and a body over the shared warmup-cast-cooldown shape in crate::entity::ai::goal::SpellcasterUseSpellBase.
