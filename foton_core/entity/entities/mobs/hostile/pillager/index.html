Pillager entity.

Vanilla parity: Pillager. The illager that shoots. Everything about it is the crossbow: it stops to wind, walks at half speed while loaded, and holds the shot for a second after the click. It also carries a five-slot bag it only ever uses during a raid, to pick the white banners it needs off the ground.
