Ravager entity.

Vanilla parity: Ravager. The siege engine of a raid: a hundred hearts, twelve damage, and a charge that chews through leaves and jumps walls. Its whole rhythm is three timers – the swing, the stun and the roar – and the interesting one is the stun: block a ravager’s hit with a shield and half the time it staggers for two seconds, then roars and throws everything around it away. That is the only opening a player gets.
