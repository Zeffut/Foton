The breeze’s brain.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.breeze.BreezeAi.

The fight activity is gated on WALK_TARGET being absent, which is what makes the breeze alternate: super::behaviors::Slide runs in the fight activity and sets a walk target, that immediately invalidates the fight activity, and the idle activity’s SlideToTargetSink is what actually walks the breeze there. Losing that condition would leave a breeze rooted to the spot.
