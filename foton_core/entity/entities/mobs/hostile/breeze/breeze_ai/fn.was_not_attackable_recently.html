Vanilla parity: the negation of Sensor.wasEntityAttackableLastNTicks(breeze, 100).

rememberPositives keeps a countdown rather than a timestamp: a target that tests attackable resets it to a hundred, and every call after that spends one. So a breeze keeps a target it has lost sight of for a hundred more brain ticks and only then gives up, which is what stops it forgetting a player who steps behind a pillar. The counter belongs to this one predicate, exactly as vanilla’s captured AtomicInteger belongs to one Breeze.
