Module behaviors

Expand description

The five behaviors only a breeze runs.

Vanilla splits these across Shoot, ShootWhenStuck, Slide, LongJump and the BreezeAi.SlideToTargetSink inner class, all in the breeze package. They are one fight – circle, shoot, jump away, shoot again – and only make sense read together, so they are one file here.

Structs§

LongJump 🔒
Crouches, then throws the breeze in an arc onto a spot behind its target.
Shoot 🔒
Inhales, then fires a wind charge.
ShootWhenStuck 🔒
Gives a pinned breeze a reason to fire.
Slide 🔒
Picks somewhere to slide to.
SlideToTargetSink 🔒
Walks the breeze to its walk target in the sliding pose.
