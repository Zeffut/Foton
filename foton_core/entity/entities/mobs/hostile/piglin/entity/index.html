Piglin entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.piglin.Piglin. The mob the Nether’s economy runs on: it wears gold, hunts hoglins in packs, is calmed by gold armor, and trades a gold ingot for whatever gameplay/piglin_bartering rolls.

Everything it does is brain-driven – see super::piglin_ai. This file is the body: the inventory it carries, the crossbow it fires, the equipment it spawns with, and the clock that turns it into a crate::entity::entities::ZombifiedPiglinEntity in the overworld.
