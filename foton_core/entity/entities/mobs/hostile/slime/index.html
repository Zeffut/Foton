Slime entity.

Vanilla parity: Slime, which is AbstractCubeMob plus four sounds and a spawn rule. The hopping, the size arithmetic and the splitting all live in super::cube_common, which is what makes this file short.

What is genuinely the slime’s own is where it appears: swamps at night, and one chunk in ten deep underground. The second is why slime farms are dug where they are.
