Illusioner entity.

Vanilla parity: Illusioner. The illager nothing spawns: it exists only behind a summon command, and it is the only illager that carries a bow. Both of its spells attack a player’s information rather than their health – one blinds, the other splits the illusioner into four decoys the client draws and the server knows nothing about.
