Sulfur cube entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.cubemob.SulfurCube. It shares super::cube_common with the slime and the magma cube, and almost nothing else: it hurts nobody by touch, it has no targets, and it is a Bucketable Shearable animal in every way that matters.

What it is really about is the block in its body. Swallow one and the cube stops moving under its own power and becomes a thing you kick around: every archetype whose item tag holds that block is applied at once, and between them they decide how it bounces, how far it slides, whether it floats, whether touching it burns, and whether it is a walking TNT block. Shears get the block back out.
