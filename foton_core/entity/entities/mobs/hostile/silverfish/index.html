Silverfish entity.

Vanilla parity: Silverfish, Silverfish.SilverfishWakeUpFriendsGoal, and Silverfish.SilverfishMergeWithStoneGoal. A silverfish wanders quietly, attacks in melee, and — in vanilla — hides inside stone-like blocks and calls nearby infested blocks for help when it is hurt.
