Drowned entity.

Vanilla parity: Drowned. A zombie that lives in water and is the reason every shoreline is dangerous at night. What makes it its own mob is that it swims: it navigates as a water creature while submerged and as a walker on land, switching between the two as it goes. Foton decides that per path request through [Mob::navigation_kind], which is exactly the seam the amphibious case needs.
