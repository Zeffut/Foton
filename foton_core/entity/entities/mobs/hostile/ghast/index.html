Ghast entity.

Vanilla parity: Ghast, Ghast.GhastShootFireballGoal and the two public goals it shares with the happy ghast. A ghast drifts through the nether on nothing but its move control, faces wherever it is heading, and lobs a fireball that a player can bat straight back into it.

Gaps: Ghast.getMaxSpawnClusterSize caps a natural spawn at one ghast, and Foton’s natural spawner has no per-entity cluster-size hook to hang that on yet.
