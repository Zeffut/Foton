Module guardian_common 

Source
Expand description

What a guardian and an elder guardian share.

Vanilla parity: Guardian, minus the handful of overrides ElderGuardian makes. Foton has no entity class hierarchy, so the whole of the guardian – its goal set, its beam, its thorns, the way it flops on land and swims in water – lives here as free functions over GuardianLike, and the two mobs differ only in the values they answer.
