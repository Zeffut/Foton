Wolf entity.

Vanilla parity: Wolf. The first tameable mob in Foton, and the one that exercises every part of TamableAnimal: it is tamed with bones, it sits where it is told, it wears a dyed collar, it fights whoever fights its owner, and it is a NeutralMob on top of all that – a wild wolf hit once keeps hunting the attacker long after losing sight of them.
