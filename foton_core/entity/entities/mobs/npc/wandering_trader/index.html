The wandering trader.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.npc.wanderingtrader.WanderingTrader.

It trades like a villager and nothing else about it is the same: it never levels up, never restocks, shows no experience bar, and leaves after its despawn delay runs out. Its stock is drawn once from three pools – the things it buys, an uncommon and a common set – and that is all it will ever sell.
