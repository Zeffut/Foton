The behaviors that get a villager to a workstation and keep it there.

Vanilla parity: SetWalkTargetFromBlockMemory, GoToPotentialJobSite, AssignProfessionFromJobSite, ResetProfession, YieldJobSite, PoiCompetitorScan and WorkAtPoi, all of which vanilla types to Villager even though they sit in the shared ai/behavior package.
