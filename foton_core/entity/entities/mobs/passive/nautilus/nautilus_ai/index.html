The nautilus’s brain.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.nautilus.NautilusAi. Three activities: a core that panics, swims and counts three cooldowns down, an idle set that courts, follows food and picks a fight, and a fight set that is one behavior – the charge.
