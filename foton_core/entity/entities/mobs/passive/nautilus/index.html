The nautilus family.

Vanilla parity: the net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.nautilus package. Both mobs share the AbstractNautilus layer, so they are grouped the way vanilla groups them rather than split across Foton’s passive/hostile folders – the zombie nautilus is a MONSTER by category and still belongs here, the same way the zombie horse sits with the equines.
