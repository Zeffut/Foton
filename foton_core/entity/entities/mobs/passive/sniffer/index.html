Sniffer entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.sniffer.Sniffer. A sniffer is a walking loot table with a six-step ritual in front of it: it scents, sniffs, picks a patch of ground it has not dug before, walks there, searches, digs, and leaves an ancient seed behind. Breeding a pair gives a sniffer egg rather than a calf, and that egg is what hatches the next one – the loop this closes.
