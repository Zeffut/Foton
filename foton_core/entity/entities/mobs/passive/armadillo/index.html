Armadillo entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.armadillo.Armadillo. An armadillo balls up when something frightens it – an undead, whatever hit it, or a player sprinting or riding past – and while it is balled up it takes half the damage, cannot be bred, and refuses every interaction but a brush. It sheds a scute every five to ten minutes on its own, and a brush takes one early.
