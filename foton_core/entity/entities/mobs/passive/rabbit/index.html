Rabbit entity.

Vanilla parity: Rabbit. A rabbit never walks. Vanilla swaps out both its jump control and its move control so that every step is a hop, and the two collaborate: the move control decides how fast the next hop will be, the jump control fires it, and customServerAiStep decides when the rabbit is allowed to hop again. That trio is what this module is mostly about; the variants, the carrot raiding and the breeding sit on top of it.
