Module bee

Expand description

Bee entity.

Vanilla parity: Bee. A bee is built around two block positions it remembers – a hive and a flower – and everything it does is a trip between them. It leaves the hive, finds a flower, hovers over it until it is carrying nectar, grows whatever crop it happens to fly over on the way, and comes back to raise the hive’s honey level. Provoke it and it stings once, which fills the target with poison and kills the bee within the minute.

Foton already had beehives, bee nests, honey levels, comparator output and harvesting; this is the occupant those blocks were written for.
