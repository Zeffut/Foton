Polar bear entity.

Vanilla parity: PolarBear. What makes a polar bear more than a large cow is that it is only dangerous because of its cubs: an adult on its own ignores players, an adult with a cub within eight blocks hunts them, and a cub that is hurt calls the adults in and then runs. Before it strikes it rears up, which is the standing flag the client animates.
