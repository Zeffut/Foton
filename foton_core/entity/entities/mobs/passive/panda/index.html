Panda entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.panda.Panda. A panda is an Animal with a pair of genes, and what those two genes come out as is the whole mob: a lazy one lies on its back, a worried one bolts from players and sits out thunderstorms, a playful one rolls downhill, a weak one sneezes as a cub and drops a slime ball doing it, an aggressive one bites back, and a brown one is only brown. Everything below is one of those seven.
