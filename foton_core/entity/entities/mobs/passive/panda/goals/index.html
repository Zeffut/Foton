The panda’s goals.

Vanilla parity: the ten inner classes of Panda. Most of them are an ordinary goal with panda.canPerformAction() bolted onto canUse, which Rust expresses as a wrapper rather than a subclass; the four that are really new – sit, lie on back, roll and sneeze – are written out.
