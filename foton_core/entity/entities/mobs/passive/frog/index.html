Frog entity.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.frog.Frog. A frog is an Animal driven by a brain rather than a goal selector: it hops in long arcs toward lily pads, swims, croaks, swallows small magma cubes whole – which is where a froglight comes from – and, once bred, walks to the bank and lays the frogspawn a tadpole hatches out of.
