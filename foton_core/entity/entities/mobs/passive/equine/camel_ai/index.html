The camel’s brain, shared by the camel and the camel husk.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.camel.CamelAi. Two activities, and what makes it a camel rather than a horse is RandomSitting – an idle camel that has been in one pose for twenty seconds flips to the other, which is why a wild camel is usually found sitting down.

CamelHusk inherits this whole brain unchanged, AnimalMakeLove on EntityType.CAMEL included. That behavior is inert for a husk, which cannot fall in love at all, exactly as it is in vanilla.
