Cat entity.

Vanilla parity: Cat. A tameable animal that is tamed with fish rather than bones, and whose whole character is where it chooses to sit: on a chest, on a lit furnace, at the foot of a bed, or on the owner who is asleep in it – and, when that owner wakes, the present it leaves behind.
