The tempt goal a stray cat follows fish with.

Vanilla parity: Cat.CatTemptGoal, an inner class of Cat. It adds two things to TemptGoal: it only runs while the cat is untamed, and it picks one player at random that it will not be scared of – which is what lets a player who stands still eventually get close enough to feed it.
