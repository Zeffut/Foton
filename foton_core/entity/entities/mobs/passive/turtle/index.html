Turtle entity.

Vanilla parity: Turtle. A turtle is built around one place: the beach it hatched on. It swims wherever it likes, but a pregnant turtle walks back to that beach, digs, and lays the eggs the next generation hatches from. Almost every goal here exists to serve that round trip, and the egg it lays is the TurtleEggBlock whose hatch path spawns turtles again.
