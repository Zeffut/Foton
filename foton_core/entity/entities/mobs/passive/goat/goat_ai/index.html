The goat’s brain.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.goat.GoatAi. Four activities: the shared core, an idle set, the long jump, and the ram.

A goat is the one vanilla animal with no registerGoals at all – every thing it does lives here, which is why Foton’s goat stood still until this landed.
