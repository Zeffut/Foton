pub fn throw_target(body: &dyn LivingEntity, target: &SharedEntity)

Launches target away from body.

Vanilla parity: HoglinBase.throwTarget. Vanilla feeds nextInt(21) - 10 straight into Vec3.yRot, which takes radians, so the push is swung by up to ten radians rather than ten degrees – effectively a random direction. It is ported as written, because that scatter is what the hit looks like in game.
