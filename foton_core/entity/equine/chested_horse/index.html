Shared vanilla AbstractChestedHorse state and hooks.

Vanilla parity: AbstractChestedHorse. The chest is what separates a donkey from a horse: strapping one on grows the mob’s own inventory instead of opening a block, so the container has to survive a save on the entity and be rebuilt whenever the chest comes or goes.
