Shared vanilla layers of the animal.equine package.

Vanilla parity: AbstractHorse, AbstractChestedHorse and Llama. Every horse-shaped mob sits on AbstractHorse: it is what carries the saddle, the temper that decides when a wild horse gives up bucking, the rearing, the steering seam a rider uses, and the inventory that survives a save. The chest and the caravan are the two layers built on top of it, and both are shared by more than one mob, which is why they live here rather than in a single entity’s file.
