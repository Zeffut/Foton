Bucking a rider off an untamed horse.

Vanilla parity: RunAroundLikeCrazyGoal. This is the whole taming loop: a wild horse bolts with whoever climbed on, and every so often rolls its temper against a random number. Win and the horse is tamed, lose and the rider is thrown, five temper richer.
