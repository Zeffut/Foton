Turning a ghast to face where it is going.

Vanilla parity: Ghast.GhastLookGoal and the Ghast.faceMovementDirection static it calls, both public and both shared with the happy ghast. A ghast has no look control worth the name – it simply points its whole body along its heading, or at its target when it has one.
