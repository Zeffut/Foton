Drifting somewhere else for no reason at all.

Vanilla parity: Ghast.RandomFloatAroundGoal, a public goal shared by the ghast and the happy ghast. It is the whole of a ghast’s idle movement: pick a point within sixteen blocks, hand it to the move control, and let the move control coast there.
