Something that periodically writes what it observes into a brain.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.ai.sensing.Sensor. Vanilla’s tick is final and owns the scan-rate countdown; here the countdown lives in the brain so the trait stays a single overridable surface.
