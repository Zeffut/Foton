The two behaviors that carry a mob’s inventory to somebody else.

Vanilla parity: GoAndGiveItemsToTarget and StayCloseToTarget. They are a pair: the first walks up to the deposit point and throws one item at it, and the second is what keeps the mob near that point while its hands are empty.
