The long jump: LongJumpUtil, LongJumpToRandomPos, LongJumpToPreferredBlock and LongJumpMidJump.

Vanilla splits these across four files, but they are one mechanism – a mob picks a landing spot it cannot walk to, solves a ballistic arc that clears everything between, crouches for two seconds and throws itself – and the three behaviors only make sense read together.
