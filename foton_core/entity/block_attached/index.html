Shared behavior for entities hung on a block.

Vanilla parity: BlockAttachedEntity. Item frames, paintings and the leash knot are not living entities, so the ordinary damage path refuses them and they would be indestructible. What breaks them instead is this: any hit that gets past the invulnerability check removes the entity outright and asks it what to leave behind.

Each concrete hanging entity performs Vanilla’s periodic support check in its own tick implementation and emits a cancellable physics break event.
