An entity that hangs on a block.

Vanilla parity: BlockAttachedEntity.

Required Methods§

Source

fn drop_item(&self, world: &World, caused_by: Option<&SharedEntity>)

Leaves behind whatever breaking this entity yields.

Vanilla parity: the abstract BlockAttachedEntity.dropItem. caused_by is the entity ultimately responsible, which decides whether anything drops at all – a creative player gets nothing back.

Source

fn hurt_block_attached(&self, world: &World, source: &DamageSource) -> bool

Takes a hit, removing this entity and dropping what it leaves.

Vanilla parity: BlockAttachedEntity.hurtServer. The damage amount is ignored on purpose: one hit of any size takes the whole thing down.
