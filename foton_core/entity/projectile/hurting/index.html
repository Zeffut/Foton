Vanilla AbstractHurtingProjectile – the self-propelled projectile loop.

What sets these apart from a thrown snowball is that they carry their own thrust. Every tick the projectile adds acceleration_power along its own heading before drag is applied, so a fireball crosses a room at the speed it left the ghast instead of arcing to the floor; gravity never enters into it.
