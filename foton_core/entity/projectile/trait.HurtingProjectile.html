fn hurting_projectile_tick(&self)

Vanilla parity: AbstractHurtingProjectile.tick.

Reached from a subclass’s tick. Thrust and drag, then the move-vector raycast, then the move, then the Projectile base tick, then the hit.

Source

fn hurting_projectile_on_deflection(&self, by_attack: bool)

Vanilla parity: AbstractHurtingProjectile.onDeflection.

A projectile batted away by an attack gets its full thrust back; one merely bounced off a shield keeps half, so it falls short.
