Shared vanilla AbstractNautilus state and hooks.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.animal.nautilus.AbstractNautilus. A nautilus is the game’s only tameable mount that lives underwater: it is tamed with food, saddled, ridden with the mouse rather than with a walk input, dashes when the rider charges a jump, and keeps its rider breathing while they sit on it. Both the nautilus and the zombie nautilus are this class plus a sound table, so everything here is shared.
