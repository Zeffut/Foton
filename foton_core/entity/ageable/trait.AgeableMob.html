fn can_be_a_baby(&self) -> bool

Returns whether this mob has a baby form at all.

Vanilla parity: AgeableMob.canBeABaby. A camel husk answers false: there is no young husk, so it never reads as a baby, setBaby does nothing to it, and its age is not saved.
