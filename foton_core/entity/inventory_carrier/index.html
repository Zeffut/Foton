Mobs that carry a container of their own.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.entity.npc.InventoryCarrier. The piglin and the villager already carried one each with their own copy of the save code; this is the shared interface behind them, and it is what lets a brain behavior reach an inventory whose shape it does not know.
