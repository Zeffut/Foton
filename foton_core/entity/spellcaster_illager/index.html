The illagers that cast.

Vanilla parity: SpellcasterIllager. Two mobs share it, the evoker and the illusioner, and between them they have five spells. The class itself is the rhythm all five run on: a warmup during which the caster stands still with its arms up, a moment where the spell fires, and a cooldown before the next one. A spell is therefore three numbers and a body, which is exactly the shape crate::entity::ai::goal::SpellcasterUseSpellBase takes.
