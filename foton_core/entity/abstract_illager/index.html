The four illagers, and what they have in common.

Vanilla parity: AbstractIllager. Very little of the class is behavior – it adds one arm pose, one alliance rule and one door goal on top of crate::entity::Raider – but the alliance rule is what keeps a pillager’s bolt from killing the vindicator standing in front of it, and the arm pose is the whole of what a player sees an illager doing.
