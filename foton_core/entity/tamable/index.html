Shared vanilla TamableAnimal state and hooks.

Vanilla parity: TamableAnimal and OwnableEntity. A tameable animal is an Animal that remembers one player, obeys a sit order, and teleports to its owner when it falls too far behind. Wolf, cat and parrot all sit on this, and none of them behaves right without it: the sit order, the collar owner check and the “who may I attack” rule are all decided here rather than per mob.
