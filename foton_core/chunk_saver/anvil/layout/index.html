Finding the dimensions inside a vanilla world folder.

Vanilla 26.x keeps every dimension under dimensions/<namespace>/<path>/. Before that the overworld sat at the world root and the other two in DIM-1 and DIM1; Paper additionally splits them across separate world folders, each in the old shape. The legacy shapes are recognized so a world that was already upgraded in place still imports, but only the DataVersion decides whether a world is current: see super::ImportError::DataVersion.
