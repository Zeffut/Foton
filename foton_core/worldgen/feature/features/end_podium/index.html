The exit portal at the center of the End.

Vanilla parity: EndPodiumFeature. The bedrock pillar with the four torches that the dragon dies over, and the portal home that opens inside it.

This is not a worldgen feature in the ordinary sense. Nothing places it during chunk generation: EnderDragonFight builds it directly into a live level, inactive when the world is first entered and active once the dragon is dead. So it takes a World rather than a WorldGenRegion, and it is not registered in the configured-feature tables.

location is the other half of its job, and the more widely used one: it is what the dragon, its phases and the fight all aim at.
