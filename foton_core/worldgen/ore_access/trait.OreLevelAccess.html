Expand description

Ore-vein reads and writes, separated from the level surface underneath them.

Vanilla parity: BulkSectionAccess, which OreFeature builds over the level it places into so a vein touches each chunk section once instead of once per block. A generation region owns a section cache and can do exactly that. A live world cannot hold sections open across a whole vein – everything else in the tick shares them – so LevelOreAccess writes through the level one block at a time.
