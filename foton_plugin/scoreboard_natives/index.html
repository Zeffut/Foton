org.bukkit.scoreboard.Team, answered by the scoreboard /team uses.

Every native names a world and reaches that world’s domain scoreboard, the one selectors, friendly fire and /team already read. A change queues the team packet vanilla would send; the server sends it to the domain’s players on its next tick, which is what makes a prefix appear in game.
