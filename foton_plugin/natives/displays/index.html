Module displays 

Source
Expand description

Display entities and the interaction entity: org.bukkit.entity.Display and its block, item and text kinds, and Interaction.

Everything here is synced entity data. The client interpolates a display between the values it is sent, so setting the interpolation duration and delay, or the teleport duration, is the whole of the animation: one packet of metadata and the client does the rest.
