AttributeInstance: an entity’s attribute, keyed the way vanilla keys it.

Since 1.21 a modifier is identified by a namespaced key, which is what Paper’s AttributeModifier carries and what Foton’s AttributeMap stores, so the key crosses unchanged – a modifier a plugin adds under zeldaciv:goron_speed is the one /attribute lists under that name.
