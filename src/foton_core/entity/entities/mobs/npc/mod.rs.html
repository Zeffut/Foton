5//! # What a Foton villager does
6//!
7//! It has a biome variant, a profession and a level; it walks to an unclaimed
8//! workstation and takes its trade from it; it claims a bed and a meeting
9//! point; it rolls the trades its profession and level call for out of the
10//! `villager_trade` data registry; it opens the trading screen, banks
11//! experience, levels up, restocks twice a day, and remembers who cured it,
12//! traded with it or hit it.
13//!
14//! It also has a **day**: the `villager_schedule` timeline steps it between the
15//! IDLE, WORK, MEET, PLAY and REST activities as the clock turns, and it
16//! panics when something dangerous comes near. [`villager_ai`] holds those
17//! packages and lists, package by package, the behaviors that are still
18//! missing -- chiefly farming, breeding, the bell, and everything a raid
19//! touches.
