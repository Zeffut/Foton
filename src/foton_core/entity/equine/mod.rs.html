1//! Shared vanilla layers of the `animal.equine` package.
2//!
3//! Vanilla parity: `AbstractHorse`, `AbstractChestedHorse` and `Llama`. Every
4//! horse-shaped mob sits on [`AbstractHorse`]: it is what carries the saddle,
5//! the temper that decides when a wild horse gives up bucking, the rearing, the
6//! steering seam a rider uses, and the inventory that survives a save. The
7//! chest and the caravan are the two layers built on top of it, and both are
8//! shared by more than one mob, which is why they live here rather than in a
9//! single entity's file.
