1//! The behaviors a raid drives on the village it besieges.
2//!
3//! Vanilla parity: the raid half of `net.minecraft.world.entity.ai.behavior` --
4//! `SetRaidStatus`, `ResetRaidStatus`, `ReactToBell`, `RingBell`,
5//! `LocateHidingPlace`, `SetHiddenState` and `MoveToSkySeeingSpot`.
6//!
7//! Vanilla types all seven on `LivingEntity` rather than on `Villager`, and
8//! none of them reads anything only a villager has, so they sit with the rest
9//! of the framework; the activity packages that schedule them are the
10//! villager's own. `CelebrateVillagersSurvivedRaid` is the exception -- vanilla
11//! types that one on `Villager` too -- and lives beside those packages instead.
12//!
13//! # How a village learns it is under attack
14//!
15//! [`SetRaidStatus`] runs in the core package, so it runs whatever the villager
16//! is doing: once a raid covers the villager's block it forces `PRE_RAID` while
17//! the countdown runs and RAID once the first wave is on the ground, and makes
18//! that the *default* activity so the schedule cannot pull the villager back to
19//! bed. [`ResetRaidStatus`] sits at the bottom of both of those packages and
20//! hands the day back when the raid stops or the village loses.
21//!
22//! The bell is the other way in. [`ReactToBell`] reads the `HEARD_BELL_TIME` a
23//! rung bell writes on everything nearby and sends a villager that is *not*
24//! already in a raid into HIDE; [`RingBell`] is how a villager that has been
25//! herded to the meeting point by `PRE_RAID` rings it in the first place.
26

28mod hide;
29mod raid_status;
30mod sky;
