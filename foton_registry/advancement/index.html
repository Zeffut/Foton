Module advancement 

Source
Expand description

Vanilla advancement definitions and the per-player progress over them.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.advancements. The definitions are generated from the built-in datapack by foton-registry/build/advancements.rs, so everything here is plain data plus the codecs the protocol needs. The tree, the per-player bookkeeping and the triggers live in foton-core.
