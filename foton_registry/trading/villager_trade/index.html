One entry of the villager_trade data registry, and the price it resolves to.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.item.trading.VillagerTrade and its TradeCost. Since 26.2 a trade is data, not code: everything a villager is willing to swap lives in data/minecraft/villager_trade/**, and building an offer means running that entry’s loot functions over the item it gives.
