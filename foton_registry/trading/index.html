Module trading 

Source
Expand description

Merchant trades: what a villager or a wandering trader is willing to swap.

Vanilla parity: net.minecraft.world.item.trading. The half that lives here is the half the protocol and the trading menu both need – a price, an offer, and a merchant’s list of them. The half that builds those offers out of the villager_trade and trade_set data registries is a separate concern and does not belong in the wire types.
