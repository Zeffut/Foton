Placing rolled loot into a container.

Vanilla parity: LootTable.fill and the two helpers it is built on, shuffleAndSplitItems and getAvailableSlots. Between them they are what makes a dungeon chest look like a dungeon chest: the stacks are broken up and scattered over the free slots instead of being packed into the first few.
