HOW A REPORT GETS HERE

Typing it should be easier than remembering it.

A test session is worth exactly what the testers noticed, and most of that is lost in the gap between noticing something and writing it down somewhere else. So /bug opens a real form on the player's screen — a category to pick and a box to describe what happened. /report and /bugreport open the same one, because a tester types the word that comes to mind and a wrong guess should not cost the report.

Everything that can be captured without asking is captured: who filed it, which world, the exact position, and the build it was filed against. A reporter writes the one thing a server cannot work out on its own — what they expected to happen instead.
